# Success Criteria

## User Success

- Efficiency: Heirs and lawyers complete three-generation Manasikhat cases in under five minutes.
- Trust: Every exported report includes direct fiqh citations. The PRD's "Mawareeth-Certified" phrasing for every report conflicts with the dual-state model and remains an open question in [SPEC.md](./SPEC.md).
- Usability: Non-experts can use the guided interview to define complex family trees accurately.

## Business Success

- Market dominance: Reach and maintain the number 1 global search ranking for Islamic inheritance calculators.
- Authority: Secure formal endorsement from at least one recognized Lebanese Islamic Council.
- Adoption: Achieve 500 or more unique calculations within 30 days of launch.
- Marketplace supply: Activate at least 50 approved lawyers within the first 6 months.
- Marketplace demand: Complete at least 100 paid certification orders within the first 90 days after marketplace launch.

## Technical Success

- Accuracy: Achieve a 100% pass rate on a 1,000-plus scholarly validated case suite.
- Integrity: Prevent logic leaks between the Python 3.12 domain layer and the Django persistence layer.
- Performance: Keep API responses for complex graph traversals below 1 second.
