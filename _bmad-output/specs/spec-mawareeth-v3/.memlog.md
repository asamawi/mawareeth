- (capability) CAP-4 lets eligible lawyers join a controlled marketplace, publish certification pricing, and be compared only within compliant disclosure rules.
- (capability) CAP-5 lets a user purchase lawyer certification and receive an auditable certified result without any lawyer overriding the engine math.
- (capability) CAP-6 lets users handle sensitive inheritance cases with privacy-first defaults while still supporting encrypted persistent records for certified cases.
- (constraint) Phase 1 remains the Heroku-to-Hetzner migration of the existing Django monolith, and this product contract cannot make frontend or backend modernization a prerequisite for that phase.
- (constraint) The product must preserve dual states of preliminary and certified outputs, with non-removable disclaimers on non-certified results.
- (constraint) Only platform-approved lawyers may appear in certification flows or issue certified reports.
- (constraint) Lawyer pricing must be disclosed clearly before purchase, sponsored placements must be labeled, and public reviews must come only from verified completed certification orders.
- (constraint) Calculation purity is non-negotiable: no workflow may manually override the engine mathematical output.
- (constraint) Accessibility and mobile performance are mandatory, including WCAG 2.1 AA support, high-legibility Arabic typography, and acceptable behavior on 3G and 4G networks.
- (constraint) Persisted case data must use encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, and QR-verifiable report authenticity.
- (constraint) Target response times remain under 200 ms for standard cases and under 1 s for complex Manasikhat traversals.
