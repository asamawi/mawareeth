# Reconcile: deep-research-report-2 (international market entry) vs PRD + addendum

Paid review, Ja'fari and outside review are deliberately out of scope and are not listed.

- [gap] In diaspora jurisdictions (UK, Canada, Germany, France, US) civil law overrides: forced heirship (French réserve héréditaire, German Pflichtteil), intestacy, family-provision claims, EU succession rules. A raw faraid result is not the legally distributable outcome. → PRD adds German, Spanish, Italian, Turkish and English-speaking diaspora Locales, but Country notices (FR-19) exist only for the seven Country laws. No generic "civil-law limits apply where you live / a local lawyer decides how this is implemented" notice for School-basis cases or diaspora locales. (Report 2 "Five-market screening", "Fictional cases"; PRD §4.5 FR-19, §4.6)
- [gap] "Gross wealth is not the estate available for faraid": joint tenancy, beneficiary designations and probate valuation. → FR-9 touches this ("jointly owned or beneficiary-designated assets may not form part of the estate"). It is only a hint, not a gate: a PDF assumption line saying a solicitor or lawyer supplied or verified the figure is missing, and nothing handles a Locale-appropriate wording per jurisdiction. (Report 2 "After death", "Fictional cases"; PRD FR-9)
- [gap] The professional-facing handoff artefact: assumptions sheet, exact fractions, school comparison, successive-death schedule (death one, carried fraction, second estate, final positions), reconciliation to one whole, and a footer that the estate figure and heir eligibility were supplied by the instructing party. → PRD gives only a PDF header (FR-13) and a footer (FR-9). No assumptions sheet, no per-stage reconciliation, no matter/client reference field. Competitors sell exactly this as a £29 "solicitor instruction letter". (Report 2 "Proposed offer", "Fictional sample deliverable", benchmark table; PRD FR-9, FR-13, FR-6)
- [gap] French locale. Report 2 says the French route showed an older "2.0" product that redirected into an English login flow. Addendum D records this and a live recheck. → PRD itself has no acceptance criterion for an end-to-end French interview. FR-20 covers the nine new Locales only, FR-25 is a copy pass, and the recheck exists only in the addendum. (Report 2 "What is verified live"; addendum D, PRD FR-20, FR-25)
- [gap] Expose the production commit through a non-sensitive `/version` endpoint so source findings can be tied to what is deployed. → Not in PRD. NFR-2 and FR-31 depend on "engine version" with no way to see it. (Report 2 "Commit and deployment limitation"; PRD NFR-2, FR-31)
- [gap] Source defaults to Hanbali where a school-dependent decision is not selected. Several presets in the addendum have an unnamed default ("Sharia, unnamed" for the UAE and Algeria, "law's own preferences" for Saudi). → Addendum A does not say which concrete School the engine uses when a preset's default is unnamed. Each such preset needs a named fallback or it silently inherits the engine default. (Report 2 "What is verified in source"; addendum A "Default school for gaps")
- [gap] Pre-death use: families and professionals use calculators for wills and planning, and the report scopes the pre-death handoff out. → PRD §5 excludes will drafting but says nothing about a user who is planning (living deceased). The interview assumes a death has occurred. (Report 2 "Before death"; PRD §5)
- [gap] Limits visible in source: 50 heirs per guest case and at most two later deaths; 24-hour idle expiry. → PRD states 50 heirs and 24 hours but not the two-later-deaths cap. That cap matters for the API (FR-31) and the OpenAPI document (FR-32). (Report 2 "What is verified in source"; PRD §3 Case, FR-31)
- [gap] Locale/country mismatch: UJ-4 has Bilal reading Urdu about a death in Lahore, and Turkish, Indonesian and Malay are shipped as Locales. The report shows jurisdiction, not language, drives which rules apply. → No preset for Pakistan, Turkey, Indonesia or Malaysia, and nothing tells those users their Locale does not select a national law. §5 only says "no presets beyond the seven". (Report 2 "Five-market screening"; PRD UJ-4, §4.5, §5)
- [gap] Out-of-scope triggers for a custom route: unclear marriage or divorce status, trusts, disputed ownership, contested heirship, cross-border governing law. → Not flagged to the user in the interview or results. (Report 2 "Proposed offer as the customer sees it"; PRD FR-16)
- [gap] Tone and trust: "reviewed, not certified"; trust is core; stop-rules that count any repeated substantive engine error against the offer. → PRD §9 and SM-C1 carry the spirit. Missing is a user-visible correction path (report an engine error from a result), which the report treats as the main trust mechanism. (Report 2 "Falsifiable thresholds"; PRD SM-C1, §9)
