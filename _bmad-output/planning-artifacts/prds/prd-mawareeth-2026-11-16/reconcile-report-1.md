# Reconcile: deep-research-report-1 (Saudi commercial opportunity) vs PRD + addendum

Paid review, Ja'fari and outside review are deliberately out of scope and are not listed.

- [gap] The sample deliverables show a traceable chain a lawyer can audit: death one, share carried forward, second estate, final reconciliation, and a check that the fractions sum to 864/864. "The actual value is not another result table but a traceable chain." → PRD has no requirement for a carry-forward ledger, a per-layer reconciliation line, or a "shares sum to the whole" check in proof/PDF. Addendum D only reuses the 864/864 figures as test fixtures. Related open items 3.1b/3.1c (layered proof, scholars' table for successive deaths) are not carried into the PRD. (Report 1 "Fictional sample deliverable", "Independent fictional calculation cases"; PRD FR-13, §6.1, addendum D)
- [gap] Each pack carries an unresolved-facts / assumptions list (confirm debts, funeral costs and bequests were deducted; confirm SW was married to S at death; confirm W is the same person in both layers). → PRD FR-9 adds only an estate hint and a PDF footer saying the family and amount were user-supplied. No "facts to confirm" or assumptions block in the free draft PDF, no linked-identity confirmation. (Report 1 "Fictional sample deliverable", "Pilot operating flow"; PRD FR-9)
- [gap] Saudi legal-practice law places "what Saudi law means for their specific estate" with a licensed lawyer; software should do mechanical and informational tasks and avoid stating that an heir legally qualifies in a contested case. → PRD JTBD promises "under the law that actually applies where we live" and ships a Saudi preset with statute citations. §9 only bans the words "certified/court-approved". No guardrail on advice-like wording (eligibility, contested facts, validity of wills or waqf), and no Saudi legal-practice-law note. (Report 1 "Legal, privacy, and licensing constraints"; PRD §2.1, §9, FR-13)
- [gap] Rounding: the fixtures need money rounding while exact fractions are preserved (198,333.33 and 99,166.66 against a 720,000 total). → FR-17 and NFR-2 say only "minor-unit rules" and "exact fractions unaffected". No rule for where remainder cents go or that displayed amounts sum to the estate, which matters most for a currency with no minor units. (Report 1 "two-death munasakhat case"; PRD FR-17, NFR-2)
- [gap] Production provenance could not be proven. Report 1 recommends exposing the deployed commit; Report 2 proposes a `/version` endpoint. → PRD requires determinism "for the same engine and preset version" (NFR-2, FR-31) but never says the engine version or commit appears in API output, PDF or a public endpoint. Preset version is covered (FR-14); engine version is not. (Report 1 "Repository state and production linkage"; PRD NFR-2, FR-14, FR-31)
- [gap] AGPL s13: a hosted API built by modifying the AGPL code triggers the source-offer duty. Do not assume dual-licensing rights without a contributor agreement. → NFR-5 says "all code stays AGPL" and the source offer must remain possible. It does not say where billable API keys, quotas and usage metering live (public repo or private), and it omits the contributor-license point that phase 1 relied on. (Report 1 "Repository license"; PRD NFR-5, FR-35, FR-36)
- [gap] PDPL: pseudonymise parties, avoid collecting national IDs and other documents, define controller and processor, set a short deletion period, and have a privacy lawyer review the notice, lawful basis and cross-border hosting. → FR-24 and NFR-4 cover the content of the privacy page. There is no controller/processor statement, no review step for the notice, no hosting-location statement, and retention is defined only for Tickets (FR-30), not for saved cases or guest sessions. (Report 1 "Legal, privacy, and licensing constraints"; PRD FR-24, FR-30, NFR-4)
- [gap] Free Saudi substitutes (Infath: munasakhat, direct distribution, compensation to heirs; MOJ Estate Platform: agreed division) mean the moat is explanation quality and handoff. The product should complement the official routes. → PRD has no competitive positioning, no pointer or handoff to official channels, and does not say property allocation or compensation is a non-goal (§5 lists valuation, not allocation scenarios). (Report 1 "Saudi demand", "Competitor benchmark"; PRD §1, §5)
- [gap] The existing disclaimer also tells users to have the result reviewed by a qualified scholar, court or lawyer. This is the handoff point. → PRD §1 keeps "calculation aid, not a fatwa" but drops the "get it reviewed" direction. UJ-1 and UJ-5 do not show it. (Report 1 "Product, source, and calculation verification"; PRD §1, §9)
- [misstatement] Mushtaraka fixture: Saudi law and Hanafi/Hanbali give the full brother 0 (SAR 360,000: 180,000 / 60,000 / 60,000 each maternal brother). Maliki/Shafi'i share 1/9 each (40,000). → Addendum D lists the fixture, and A lists Saudi "no sharing", consistent. But the addendum does not record the school-by-school expected results, so the fixture is not actually reusable as written. (Report 1 "Sunni-school disagreement"; addendum A, D)
- [gap] The offer's exclusions are a useful list of cases to flag in the interview: contested heirship, unclear marriage or divorce status, disputed ownership, wills/waqf validity, kinship disputes. → FR-16 covers only rules the preset lacks. Nothing flags factual-eligibility uncertainty to the user before a number is shown. (Report 1 "Customer-facing offer"; PRD FR-16)
