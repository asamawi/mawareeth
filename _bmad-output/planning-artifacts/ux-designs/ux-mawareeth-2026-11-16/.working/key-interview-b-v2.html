Interview, layout B (v2)

Relationship buttons and heir list beside a tree. No basis step: the result is the same in all four Sunni schools unless a country's law would change it, and then a prompt appears. All numbers are illustrative.

Card states

  • KarimSonDied before

    Dashed card: died before the deceased. Not an heir himself, but his children may be.

  • WifeDied later · own estate ›

    Solid card, tappable: died after the deceased but before the estate was divided. Opens her own estate.

  • Full brotherNo share in this case

    Dimmed card: blocked by another heir (here, the son).
