B. Tree beside a list

A grouped panel of one-press relationship buttons (Spouse, Children, Parents, Siblings, More relatives) with count steppers sits next to the heir list, so the common case of adding many heirs is fast and does not depend on the drawing. The interactive tree sits beside or below it as confirmation and remains tappable and droppable, with the same Add relative here alternative. The share preview sits below on phone and becomes a third column on desktop.

Strengths

  • Fastest path to enter many heirs: one press per heir and a stepper for counts.
  • The list gives a precise, screen-reader-friendly record of who is entered, in addition to the tree.
  • Tree is optional to use, so a crowded or tiny tree does not block progress.
  • Three clear columns on desktop: input, picture, result.

Risks

  • Phone is a long scroll; the tree and share preview are far from the buttons, so feedback is delayed.
  • Two representations of the same data (list and tree) can feel redundant and add maintenance cost.
  • Less discoverable for missing heirs than ghost cards on the tree.
  • Grouped relationship panel is dense in translation, with longer labels in French and other languages.
