# Input reconciliation

Input references: `prd.md` and `addendum.md` are under `planning-artifacts/prds/prd-mawareeth-2026-11-16/`; `EXPERIENCE.md` and `DESIGN.md` are under `planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-mawareeth-2026-11-16/`. Only missing architectural constraints and contradictions are listed.

- **high** | `prd.md` §3 Case, NFR-4; `EXPERIENCE.md` State Patterns / Expired case | **Missing:** AD-20 stores guest families server-side without their sliding 24-hour lifetime; AD-23 cleanup omits guest Cases. | **Spine fix:** Expire guests 24 hours after last change, enforce expiry on reads/edits, delete expired family data and return no family on expired responses.
- **high** | `prd.md` FR-41 | **Missing:** Token lifetime is mentioned, but deletion of its family on first open and concurrent redemption semantics are absent. | **Spine fix:** Atomically consume the token into one guest Case, delete its payload on redemption or expiry, and reject subsequent opens.
- **high** | `prd.md` NFR-3, §7, §4.9 feature-specific NFRs | **Missing:** The 1 s budget appears only as a Redis deferral rationale; preview p95 under 1 s on a mid-range phone over 4G at 50 Heirs, API p95 under 1 s, engine under 200 ms and bounded lock waits are not binding rules. | **Spine fix:** Adopt those budgets and bound mutation/counter lock waits within the end-to-end budget, with acceptance checks across adapters.
- **high** | `EXPERIENCE.md` Component Patterns / undo-control, undo-toast; `prd.md` FR-5 | **Contradiction:** Deferred excludes multi-step Undo, but the final UX explicitly requires multi-step history; restore version checks and history ownership are also unstated. | **Spine fix:** Adopt version-checked multi-step snapshot history, persistent keyboard-accessible Undo and a toast lasting at least 10 seconds with hover/focus suspension.
- **high** | `EXPERIENCE.md` State Patterns / Offline or slow; Gaps / Deferred review findings | **Unresolved conflict:** Server-only family ownership and request-per-edit do not support the UX assumption of local offline edits and reconnect calculation; UX itself flags queue versus blocked edits as undecided. | **Spine fix:** Explicitly resolve offline queue versus blocked edits; a queue needs ephemeral drafts, ordered replay, duplicate prevention and stale-version reconciliation.
- **high** | `prd.md` FR-38, NFR-4 | **Missing:** Dropping names after response does not prohibit request, trace or error logs retaining them, or prefill storage copying transient optional names. | **Spine fix:** Prohibit names/raw family bodies in API/MCP diagnostics and define the minimum persisted prefill payload separately from transient request names.
- **high** | `prd.md` FR-9 | **Contradiction:** AD-18 defaults a missing Basis to compare, whereas legacy Cases lacking Basis must retain their School; AD-20's migration does not specify the fallback or active guest-session conversion. | **Spine fix:** Derive Basis from legacy `madhab` before using compare, including active guest sessions, and preserve School choices through rollout and rollback.
- **high** | `prd.md` FR-21; `EXPERIENCE.md` State Patterns / Refusal | **Missing:** Generic refusal codes do not define the Lebanon Sunni/Shia/other-religion input across Interview, saved family and API/MCP. | **Spine fix:** Define the minimal Deceased eligibility discriminator in the shared family schema and enforce reason-coded Lebanon refusals before returning shares.
- **medium** | `prd.md` §4.9 feature-specific NFRs, FR-41, FR-44 | **Contradiction / input tension:** The API NFR says never a database write per request, while AD-23 mandates request-time PostgreSQL writes and AD-22 creates prefill rows; FR-41/44 themselves require durable data. | **Spine fix:** Explicitly reconcile this as a write-free calculation core with bounded shell writes, or change persistence if the literal no-write requirement must hold.
- **medium** | `EXPERIENCE.md` State Patterns / Preview updating; Accessibility Floor / Live region; edit-sheet | **Missing:** Whole-family responses do not define ordering of overlapping save-on-change and preview requests; the approximately 800 ms debounce is for announcements, not a stated calculation delay. | **Spine fix:** Version preview responses, discard obsolete results, reconcile overlapping edits, preserve prior shares on pending/error and debounce only the shared announcement.
- **medium** | `EXPERIENCE.md` Accessibility Floor / Tree accessibility model, Keyboard tree navigation, Focus management; `DESIGN.md` Typography | **Missing:** Engine edit options do not establish the canonical accessible outline, shared tree/list descriptions or stable identities for focus after whole-family replacement and Undo. | **Spine fix:** Require one `treeDescription` model, stable person IDs, native-button roving focus, RTL-aware navigation, equivalent Add-relative actions and deterministic restore/remove focus targets.
- **medium** | `prd.md` §3, FR-1, FR-4; `EXPERIENCE.md` edit-sheet, UJ-2 later-death sub-flow | **Missing:** Shared validation does not bind the 50-Heir/two-later-death limits or estate-level mutation and preview addressing. | **Spine fix:** Enforce both limits centrally and define stable estate/person references, estate-targeted edits and per-estate previews in the common contract.
- **medium** | `prd.md` FR-3; `EXPERIENCE.md` edit-sheet | **Missing:** Cascading deletion lacks the pre-commit impact list needed to tell users exactly which descendants and later-death people will disappear. | **Spine fix:** Expose an engine-derived deletion impact list and bind confirmation and atomic deletion to the same Case version.
- **medium** | `prd.md` FR-12, FR-16; `addendum.md` §A / gift_offset | **Missing:** A preset gift-offset setting does not define lifetime-gift/voluntary-bequest inputs or supplied-facts metadata needed by calculation and result explanations. | **Spine fix:** Define minimum estate/branch gift-and-bequest inputs and supplied-facts metadata once, carrying them through snapshots, undo, API/MCP, proof and PDF.
- **medium** | `prd.md` FR-28; `EXPERIENCE.md` Accessibility Floor / Language markup; `DESIGN.md` Typography | **Missing:** Catalog ownership alone does not require Arabic originals beside translated relationships or preserve mixed-script language/direction metadata. | **Spine fix:** Emit translated and Arabic term segments with language/direction metadata and require bidi isolation of names, fractions and amounts in screen/PDF renderers.
- **medium** | `prd.md` FR-25, FR-26; `EXPERIENCE.md` notice, badge-beta; `DESIGN.md` Do's and Don'ts | **Missing:** AD-19 centralizes notices without binding selection predicates or non-dismissibility. | **Spine fix:** Require disclaimer on every result, applicable Country notice, civil-law notice for School Basis in de/es/it/tr/zh/en, and applicable Beta explanations, all non-dismissible.
- **medium** | `prd.md` FR-38, FR-40, FR-43 | **Missing:** Public contracts omit ambiguous/unknown-relationship errors listing possible codes, MCP fractions as text and numbers, assistant clarification/no-re-rounding instructions and usage-term attribution. | **Spine fix:** Bind these validation, representation, tool-description and attribution obligations to the shared public API/MCP contract.
- **medium** | `prd.md` FR-42, FR-43 | **Missing:** Atomic counters and revocation do not specify key quota replacing free-IP limits, revoked keys reverting to free limits, or retry-time/key-request response fields. | **Spine fix:** Define one admission policy for valid/absent/revoked keys and a common throttle response carrying retry time and the API-access Ticket route.
- **medium** | `prd.md` FR-44; §8 SM-3, SM-5, SM-C2 | **Missing:** AD-23's day/event/Locale/country keys lack defined Basis type/preset dimensions, first-preview trigger semantics, first-reply timing and delete/Undo measures needed by metrics. | **Spine fix:** Define bounded non-personal event dimensions and lifecycle deduplication/timing rules without adding person or Case identifiers to aggregate rows.
- **medium** | `prd.md` FR-34, FR-35, FR-37 | **Missing:** Ticket storage does not bind acknowledgement Locale, first-reply timestamp, tracking-free spam protection or a concrete retention setting; “the retention period” is unspecified. | **Spine fix:** Store Ticket Locale/first-reply time, use tracking-free spam controls and define closed-at retention with the 12-month assumption and matching privacy disclosure.
- **medium** | `EXPERIENCE.md` smart-prompt, UJ-1 failure; `prd.md` UJ-1 edge case | **Missing / input tension:** UX remembers explicit prompt dismissal per Case; PRD keeps an ignored prompt; the spine defines eligibility but neither dismissal state nor that distinction. | **Spine fix:** Distinguish ignored from explicitly dismissed prompts and define Case-scoped dismissal persistence across preview/results/reload while retaining the Country-law link.
- **low** | `prd.md` FR-23 | **Missing:** Country currency defaults exist but Locale fallback and persistence/precedence of an explicit user currency do not. | **Spine fix:** Define shared currency selection precedence and persistence for user choice, Country default and Locale fallback.
