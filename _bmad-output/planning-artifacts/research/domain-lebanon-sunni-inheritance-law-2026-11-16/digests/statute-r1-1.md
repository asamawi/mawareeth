## Engine implications
- Default ruleset: Hanafi, with possible overlay by Supreme Islamic Council ruling(s) (No. 46 of 2011 concerns family matters; whether it touches inheritance is UNVERIFIED).
- Do not apply the 1959 non-Muslim succession law to Muslim estates.
- Wasiyya wajiba: no evidence of adoption found; default OFF (Hanafi: predeceased son's children get nothing unless voluntary will within 1/3). Mark as unverified.
- Bequest to heir invalid without consent and 1/3 cap: standard Hanafi, consistent with the weak al-Islam snippet.
- Radd, dhawu al-arham, grandfather, mushtaraka: no Lebanon-specific deviation found; use Hanafi defaults (radd incl. to spouse not evidenced here).
- Miri land/1913 law and interfaith inheritance: not evidenced this round.
