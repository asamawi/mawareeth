## Claims

Contested heirs/shares go elsewhere (court not named on page). ## Engine implications
- Preset "Lebanon - Sunni" = plain Hanafi (asaba, no statutory obligatory bequest, no radd to spouse evidenced) - consistent with 2007 evidence only; needs statute confirmation (Sunni 1942 code / any inheritance provisions).
- Output format: heirs plus fractional shares, matching what a hasr irth states.
- Do not apply 1959 civil rules (equal shares, forced heirship) to Muslims.
- Consider warning: result is Hanafi default; sect conversion or foreign status changes applicable law.
