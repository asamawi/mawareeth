### Morocco
Source for article text below: official French translation of Law 70-03, https://www.idpbarcelona.net/docs/recerca/marroc/pdf/nor_loi_70_03.pdf | IDP Barcelona (host; includes commentary) | 2004 text | accessed 2026-11-16 | high on text, current to pre-2021 amendments only | regulatory. Arabic official PDFs (adala.justice.gov.ma, cour-constitutionnelle.ma 2021 version) found but Arabic font extraction failed.
- Art 369: grandchildren of a son OR daughter who died before or with the deceased get an obligatory bequest "dans la limite du tiers disponible"; if concurrent with a voluntary bequest within that third, art 302 (pro rata) applies.
- Art 370: bequest = share the predeceased parent would have received had they survived the deceased; third not exceeded. Commentary method in two phases (phase 1: treat the parent as dying after the ascendant, compute parent's share, distribute among the parent's heirs incl. spouse, only to fix the amount; phase 2: final partition with the bequest as a fraction of the estate). Worked example gives 49/960.
- Art 371: no bequest if they inherit from the grandparent, or received by will or gratuitous gift in life an equal value; if less, complete it; if more, the excess needs heirs' approval; if given to only some, the others get the bequest up to their share.
- Art 372: beneficiaries "les enfants de fils, les enfants de fille et les enfants de fils du fils a l'infini", male double female; each ascendant excludes own descendant but not another's; each takes only their ascendant's share. Commentary: the daughter's line is first degree only.
- Arts 348-349: three kinds of asaba; order includes 3. paternal grandfather with full/consanguine brothers; 6. "le tresor public a defaut d'heritier"; if exactly one fard heir, the remainder goes to him; if several fard heirs whose shares do not exhaust the estate, the remainder returns to them in proportion (radd; commentary example includes the wife, i.e. spouses included, denominator 24 -> 19).
- Art 351 ta'sib bi-l-ghayr (daughter with son; son's daughter with son's son at same or lower degree unless inheriting otherwise; sisters with brothers; 2:1). Art 352 sisters asaba ma'a l-ghayr with daughter/son's daughter, ranking as brothers.
- Art 353: father/grandfather with daughter: 1/6 fard + remainder asaba. Art 354: paternal grandfather with siblings: best of 1/3 or muqasama (counting the mou'adda), and with fard heirs best of 1/6, 1/3 of remainder, muqasama.
- Arts 363-368 codify special cases: chibh al-malikiyya, al-kharqa (mother 1/3, rest grandfather and sister 2:1), al-mushtaraka (art 365: uterine and full siblings share 1/3 equally per head), al-gharrawayn (366), al-mubahala (367, 6->8), al-minbariyya (368, 24->27). Akdariyya not seen in arts 363-368 (not otherwise checked).
- Maternal grandfather is not an heir (commentary to art 349). No dhawi al-arham category in art 349; treasury is last.
- Art 400: "il conviendra de se referer au rite Malikite et a l'effort jurisprudentiel (ijtihad)..."
- Missing person: arts 327 ff. (declaration of death; suspended share example) present in the PDF, not analysed.

## Engine implications
- Morocco mode = Maliki base plus: radd to all fard heirs including spouses (art 349 par. 6) before treasury; no dhawi al-arham; maternal grandfather excluded; obligatory bequest (arts 369-372) as a pre-pass: two-phase computation, cap 1/3, 2:1, daughter's line first degree only, son's line unlimited, conditions of art 371 (inherits / gift / bequest top-up), art 302 pro rata with voluntary bequests.
- Algeria mode: tanzil per arts 169-172 (same two-phase model, cap 1/3, 2:1); daughter's-children scope must be a configurable switch until the Supreme Court holding is verified; radd excludes spouses unless no other heirs (art 167); residual Sharia (art 222).
