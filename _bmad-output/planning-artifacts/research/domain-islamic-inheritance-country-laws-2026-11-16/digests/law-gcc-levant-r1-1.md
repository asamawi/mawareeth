### Oman
- Personal Status Law issued by Sultani Decree 32/97, dated 4 June 1997 (28 Muharram 1418), Sultan Qaboos; effective one month after gazette | https://sjc.gov.om/userupload/laws/(English)قانون الاحوال الشخصية.pdf | Supreme Judicial Council, Oman | 1997 | accessed 2026-11-16 | confidence high for decree text; amendments not checked | class regulatory
- Art. 229: obligatory will for "close relatives (who are not heirs)"; if no will made for them and will is to others, two-thirds of that will is returned to them (non-classical, not the Egyptian-style grandchildren model); Art. 230 subject to Book provisions | same | SJC Oman | 1997 | accessed 2026-11-16 | confidence medium (terse English translation) | class regulatory
- Art. 239 no inheritance across religions; Art. 259 radd defined as increase of Quranic heirs' shares proportionally (spouse exclusion not visible in fetched text, unverified); Art. 261 mushtaraka codified (husband 1/2, mother/grandmother 1/6, 1/3 shared by uterine and full siblings, 2:1); dhawu al-arham four categories, males=females; missing person gets share assuming alive (Art. 272) | same | SJC Oman | 1997 | accessed 2026-11-16 | confidence medium | class regulatory
- Art. 281(c): on inheritance of the daughter, grandfather and dhawu al-arham, if the DECEASED's school differs from the Law, judge applies the preponderant opinion of the deceased's school unless heirs unanimously request the Law's texts; 281(d) residual: Sharia rules most consistent with the Law. Art. 282: non-Muslims governed by own rules unless they request the Law | same | SJC Oman | 1997 | accessed 2026-11-16 | confidence high | class regulatory
