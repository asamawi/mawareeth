## Engine implications
- Jordan mode: obligatory bequest through sons only (descendants of the son, per-branch share of the predeceased parent), 1/3 cap, gift offset, not owed if they inherit, paid before voluntary bequests, 2:1 inside the group.
- UAE mode: same mechanics but includes daughter's children; killer/apostate barred.
- Radd excludes spouses when other sharers exist. Jordan 2019: lone spouse gets radd before dhawi al-arham. UAE art. 236: dhawi al-arham inherit alongside a sole spouse (spouse radd apparently not ahead of them; verify).
- No-heir estate: Jordan to Awqaf ministry, UAE charitable waqf; not a bayt al-mal radd.
- Make akdariyya/mushtaraka rule flags (UAE 239/240 explicit; Jordan 296d mushtaraka); residual madhab: Jordan Hanafi (325); UAE general Sharia (no named school found).
- Jordan apostate rule (281 ج) looks anomalous; gate behind verification flag.
