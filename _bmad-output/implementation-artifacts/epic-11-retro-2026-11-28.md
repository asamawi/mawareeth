### F1 — An acceptance axe check of Story 11.4 fails on the current tree (fix)

- **Evidence:** a fresh `npm run build`, then `npx playwright test e2e/accessibility.spec.ts --project=chromium` on 2026-11-28 gave 65 passed and 2 failed: `accessibility.spec.ts:179` "the 320px bar with text spacing, then the open sheet" in **en light** and **ar light**. Both failed at line 185 (sheet open) with `color-contrast (serious): .text-accent`.
- **History:** added by 11.4 (`b7efa8d2`), whose run saw "one failure under load". 11.5 reproduced it on its own baseline and logged it as pre-existing (`deferred-work.md:653-655`). 11.6 and 11.7 did not run this spec, so the epic closed with it failing.
- **Cross-story cause (likely, not proven):**
  - The only `.text-accent` in the sheet is 11.1's agreement line (`share-preview.tsx:389`).
  - 11.3 gave the sheet a 200 ms opacity fade-in (`globals.css:68-81`).
  - 11.4's test opens the sheet with `openPreview` (`e2e/family.ts:37-42`), which returns as soon as the heading exists.
  - A probe measured the sheet at opacity 0.77–0.99 when `openPreview` returned in 10 of 15 runs under 8 workers. After waiting on `getAnimations()`, it was 1.0 in 15 of 15.
  - Light-mode `#0f766e` has little margin above 4.5:1 on white, so a partly faded sheet can fall below it. Dark mode passes.
  - The probe did not itself reproduce the violation, so the cause is strongly indicated, not proven.
- **AC affected:** 11.4 AC5, "relevant Playwright axe and reflow checks pass" (`epics.md:1999`).
- **Lesson:** an epic-level gate should rerun every spec a story added, not only the story's own block. 11.6 and 11.7 ran `results.spec.ts`/`interview.spec.ts` only.

### F2 — On the Interview, a full refusal or a first-load failure is invisible to sighted users (fix or decide)

- **Evidence:**
  - The bar renders only when there are shares or the sheet is already open: `visible = showBar && (hasShares || open)` (`share-preview.tsx:601-606`).
  - The one status line is `sr-only` (`start-case.tsx:554`).
  - A `refused` outcome, or an online `error` before any shares, therefore shows no reason and no Retry on screen.
  - The e2e test "a failed first preview … shows no bar" pins this behaviour (`deferred-work.md:596-597`).
- **History:** 11.3 deferred it with "Review in 11.4/11.1" (`deferred-work.md:596`). Neither the 11.1 nor the 11.4 triage log mentions it, so it fell between stories.
- **AC affected:** 11.2 AC5, "failures retain the family and Retry" (`epics.md:1934`), and 11.1 AC4, "explicit refusal reasons remain distinguishable" (`epics.md:1895`). Both hold inside the sheet, but on the Interview the sheet cannot be reached in these states. Full results does show the refusal.
- **Needs an owner decision:** show the bar (or an in-flow notice with Retry) for `refused` and first-load `error`, or record the hidden state as an accepted UX deviation and reconcile the two ACs.
